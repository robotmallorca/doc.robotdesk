# 🚀 Release v0.23.0

> **September 10, 2026**

---

## ✨ New Features

### Choose which alerts you want to see

You can now decide which alerts are used in your building. Open **Settings → Alerts** and
turn on only the ones your team cares about. The ones you hide stop appearing in the
alerts dashboard, in the statistics, in the logs and in the counters, so everyone sees a
shorter list with only what matters to them.

<img src="https://github.com/robotmallorca/doc.robotdesk/releases/download/v0.23.0/01-project-configuration.png"
     alt="Alerts settings page, with one switch per alert" width="720" style="max-width:100%;height:auto;">

*Where: Settings → Alerts*

### Long names now fit in the alerts dashboard

Long names in the alerts breakdown used to run past the edge of their card and push the
layout out of shape. They are now shortened to fit, and hovering over one shows the full
name.

<img src="https://github.com/robotmallorca/doc.robotdesk/releases/download/v0.23.0/02-alerts-dashboard.png"
     alt="Alerts dashboard showing a shortened name and the full name on hover" width="720" style="max-width:100%;height:auto;">

*Where: Alerts → Dashboard*
